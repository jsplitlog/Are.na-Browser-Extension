Not signed in.

Create a token in Are.na settings.

OAuth

OAuth sign-in needs an Are.na application registered to this extension’s exact Chrome redirect URL. It is not configured in this build.

Privacy

Nothing is sent until you click a lookup. Are.na receives search terms derived from the page URL and associates that lookup with your account. Full URLs, query strings, fragments, browsing history, analytics, and telemetry are never sent.

Token safety

Personal access tokens can grant broad access to your account. By default, this extension keeps yours only for this browser session; checking “Remember” stores it on this device. Signing out removes it from the extension, but cannot revoke it at Are.na. To fully revoke access, delete the token in Are.na settings.
